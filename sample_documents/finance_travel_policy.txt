ACME CORPORATION - TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY
Finance Department, effective 15 February 2024

1. EXPENSE CLAIMS

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the date the expense was incurred.
Claims submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed under any circumstances.
All expense claims above 5000 rupees require a receipt and prior written approval.

2. TRAVEL

Domestic flights must be booked in economy class for all employees below director level.
Hotel accommodation is reimbursed up to 4000 rupees per night in metropolitan cities.
International travel requires approval from the Finance Director at least 21 days in advance.

3. MEALS

The daily meal allowance for domestic travel is 1200 rupees per day.
Alcoholic beverages are not reimbursable under this policy.

4. REMOTE WORK EQUIPMENT

Employees working remotely may claim a one-time equipment allowance of 8000 rupees.
Employees are permitted to work remotely for up to 2 days per week.

5. UNRELATED OPERATIONS

The office cafeteria operates between 08:00 and 18:00 on weekdays.
Visitor parking is located on the second level of the north garage.
