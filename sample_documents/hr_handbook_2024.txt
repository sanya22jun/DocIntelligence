ACME CORPORATION - EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Edition 2024, effective 01 January 2024

SECTION A: TIME OFF

The leave year begins on 1 January and ends on 31 December.
Full-time employees receive 15 days of paid annual leave each calendar year.
All leave requests must be submitted at least 30 days in advance of the start date.
Unused annual leave cannot be carried over and is forfeited at the end of the year.
Managers must respond to a submitted leave request within 3 working days.

SECTION B: ILLNESS

The company provides 10 days of paid sick leave annually to every full-time employee.
A doctor's note is required when an employee is absent due to illness for more than 2 consecutive days.

SECTION C: WORKING ARRANGEMENTS

Remote work is permitted for a maximum of 3 days per week, subject to manager approval.
Employees on probation are not eligible for remote work arrangements.

SECTION D: APPRAISALS

Performance reviews take place once per year, in December.
The appraisal cycle for each year closes on 31 December.
