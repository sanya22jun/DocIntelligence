ACME CORPORATION - EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY
Revision 3.1, effective 01 April 2023

1. ANNUAL LEAVE

The leave year begins on 1 April and ends on 31 March.
Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Annual leave requests must be submitted at least 14 days before the intended start date.
Unused annual leave may be carried over to the following year, up to a maximum of 5 days.
Line managers are required to approve or reject a leave request within 3 working days.

2. SICK LEAVE

Employees may take up to 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
A medical certificate is required for any sick leave absence longer than 2 consecutive days.
Sick leave does not accumulate and cannot be carried over into the next year.

3. REMOTE WORK

Employees are permitted to work remotely for up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
Remote work is not available to employees serving their probation period.

4. PERFORMANCE REVIEW

Formal performance reviews are conducted twice per year, in June and December.
The appraisal cycle for each year closes on 31 December.
